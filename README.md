## About
Mega Man X8 16-bit is a reimagining of the PS2 original with SNES styled graphics made in Godot Engine 3.5. The original fangame was created by Allyson da Paz, and is fully playable from start to finish with X. This version, called Mega Man X8 16-bit Zashiko + TagTeam mod, adds new characters and features to the base fangame. The Zashiko mod version 1.0.0.4, which this mod is built off of, added Zero and Axl as playable characters to the game, complete with their own collectibles, dialogue, and unique gameplay. In addition to small balancing tweaks, this TagTeam mod adds the feature of being able to choose one to two characters to take to a stage and switch between them on the fly, similar to the original tag team system in Mega Man X8 on the PS2.

## Features
Tag team system:
  - Built to mimic the original X8
  - Choose 1-2 characters out of X, Zero, and Axl to take to a stage
  - Switch between them on the fly
  - Each character has their own health bar
  - Dieing as one character will spawn the other in
  - Shared giga attack bars, shared lives
  - Starting health is lower to balance the two health bars
  - Heart tanks have to be rationed between the characters on your team

Tweaks:
  - Zero's saber does more damage
  - X's armor pieces are able to be collected by any character
  - Zero's weapons are able to be collected by any character
  - Zero's knuckles are able to be collected right off the bat
  - X's icarus boots function similarly to how they did in the original fangame
  - Added dialogue to secret battle for encounters with different characters
